Practice

Risk, research and data science.

Risk management defines exposures, controls, limits, and accountability. Statistics measures evidence, uncertainty, and predictive performance. Research connects both to stronger questions, transparent methods, and practical decisions.

R Risk practice

Risk management

Financial-risk analysis, controls, modelling, validation, and decision-ready reporting.

  • Liquidity & ALM: ALM and maturity ladders, LCR, NSFR, ASF/RSF, cash-flow gaps, funding stability, limits, and liquidity scenarios.
  • Credit risk: PD/LGD/EAD, expected loss, scorecards, limits, vintage and cohort monitoring, and early-warning signals.
  • Market risk: Instruments, sensitivities, VaR and Expected Shortfall foundations, limits, stress scenarios, and portfolio interpretation.
  • Enterprise & operational: Risk appetite, KRIs, controls, incidents, IT/data risk, root-cause analysis, and remediation.
  • Model & reporting risk: Assumptions, benchmarks, backtesting, monitoring, data lineage, reproducibility, and escalation.
